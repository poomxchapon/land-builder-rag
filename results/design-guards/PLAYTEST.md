# Playtest sheets: real players, pass marks from each brief

The blind review compared design documents. These sheets are for the next step: build each game and let 5 to 8 people from its real audience play it.
Every pass mark below was worked out by code (`dev/playtest.mjs`) from the brief, the measured floor and the plan. The result column is empty until people play.

## How to run one sheet

1. Build the Land from the saved plan and walk it once yourself. Fix anything that blocks the route.
2. Mark the play area on the real floor. With several players, tape one cell per player (0.6 m clear at the edge, 1 m between cells).
3. One helper per player in the area. The helper counts, and never explains the game beyond the intro card.
4. 5 to 8 players from the audience named in the brief. One play each, no practice round.
5. After the play ask two questions only: "Which of these words fits what you felt?" (a card with the 8 feelings in plain words) and "Did you feel dizzy or sick?"
6. Fill the result column the same day. A sheet passes when every Safety mark passes and at most one other mark fails.
